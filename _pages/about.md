Hello World! I am Mayank, a PhD student at the [University of Arizona](https://www.arizona.edu/). I work on AI and Natural Language Processing under the supervision of [Prof. Eduardo Blanco](https://eduardoblanco.github.io/). My current research focuses on methods that improve agentic systems, from prompts and harnesses to the topology of multi-agent systems. I've also dabbled in relation extraction previously. I'm an experienced Software/Machine Learning Engineer, having worked in the tech industry for around 8 years at [Meta](https://about.meta.com/), [Instacart](https://www.instacart.com/), and [Cisco](https://www.cisco.com/). My interest in AI research grew over those years, and I eventually returned to academia to pursue it full-time. I completed my Masters and Bachelors at the [New York University (CIMS)](https://cims.nyu.edu/dynamic/) and the [BITS Pilani - Goa Campus](https://www.bits-pilani.ac.in/goa/), respectively. I'm always open to any collaborations. So, feel free to reach out if you like my work!
